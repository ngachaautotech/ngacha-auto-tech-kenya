Vehicle electronics

Understand the system—not just the warning light.

Modern vehicles rely on control modules and communication networks. Proper diagnosis combines scan-tool information with wiring diagrams, electrical tests and the correct repair procedure.

  • CAN Bus: communication between vehicle control modules
  • ECM / ECU: engine management and related inputs/outputs
  • TCM: electronic transmission control
  • BCM: body functions such as lighting and locking
  • Immobilizer: authorized starting and anti-theft functions
